# `chpasswd`

```text
Usage: chpasswd [--md5|--encrypted]

Read user:password from stdin and update /etc/passwd

	-e,--encrypted	Supplied passwords are in encrypted form
	-m,--md5	Use MD5 encryption instead of DES
```
